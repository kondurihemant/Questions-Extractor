### [GATE 2024 (CS2), Q.50]

Q.50 
Consider 4-variable functions f1, f2, f3, f4 expressed in sum-of-minterms form as 
given below. 
f1 = ∑(0,2,3,5,7,8,11,13)  
f2 = ∑(1,3,5,7,11,13, 15)  
f3 = ∑(0,1,4,11)  
f4 = ∑(0,2,6,13)  

With respect to the circuit given above, which of the following options is/are 
CORRECT? 

(A) 
𝒀= ∑(0,1,2,11,13)  
(B) 
𝒀= Π(3,4, 5,6,7,8,9,10,12,14,15)  
(C) 
𝒀= ∑(0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7)  
(D) 
𝒀= Π(8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15)
